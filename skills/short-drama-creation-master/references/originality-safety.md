# Originality, References, And Review Safety

## Default To Original Work

Create original names, silhouettes, costumes, architecture, props, creatures, magic systems, logos, dialogue, and plot events. High-level genre traits are acceptable; distinctive protected expression is not a shortcut for design.

When the user supplies a copyrighted reference, extract non-exclusive qualities such as:

- emotional tone
- pacing pattern
- broad era or genre
- material or lighting preference
- level of realism or stylization
- camera energy

Then replace identifying names, symbols, signature costumes, unique weapons, exact compositions, lore terms, and recognizable plot beats with a coherent original design system.

## Do Not Frame Work As Review Evasion

Do not help disguise infringement, graphic harm, or prohibited content merely to pass a platform review. Reframe the underlying creative goal into genuinely original and permitted material.

Use neutral production language only when it is accurate. For example, replace explicit gore with non-graphic impact only when the scene is actually redesigned to avoid gore, not when wording is being used to conceal the same content.

## Public Figures And Real People

Treat identity, consent, defamation, endorsement, and deceptive context as separate risks. Use fictional people when a real identity is unnecessary. Clearly label reenactments or synthetic representations when the intended use requires disclosure.

## Factual And Documentary Material

- Never fabricate quotations, credentials, statistics, or witness accounts.
- Attribute archival material and verify usage rights.
- Distinguish observation, allegation, inference, and dramatization.
- Verify unstable facts with current authoritative sources.

## Sensitive Content

Prefer implication, aftermath, reaction, distance, shadow, sound, or symbolic imagery when explicit detail is unnecessary. Preserve narrative clarity without fetishizing injury, coercion, hatred, self-harm, or illegal behavior.

## Platform Requirements

Platform and model rules change. If compliance depends on exact current wording, duration, audio support, likeness policy, or content restrictions, consult current official documentation. Record the date and source in internal notes when it materially affects delivery.

## Final Originality Check

Search the script, prompts, filenames, asset labels, and document metadata for:

- protected character or franchise names
- distinctive lore terms or slogans
- copied lyrics or dialogue
- real brand marks not required by the project
- unsupported claims about platform approval

If a reference remains necessary for internal alignment, keep it in a private reference note and remove it from generation prompts and public deliverables unless the user has the relevant rights.
