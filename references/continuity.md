# Continuity

## Select The Relationship

### Continuous

Use when the next generation must look like the same unbroken event. Require a real prior result or inspectable ending frames. Preserve the state that the viewer can observe, even when it differs from the planned ending.

### Motivated Cut

Use when editing permits a new angle, insert, reaction, time transition, or location change. Preserve identity and story facts, but do not mechanically copy the previous framing. Make the cut legible through gaze, action match, sound bridge, geography, or an explicit transition.

### Independent

Use for coverage, montage units, product shots, B-roll, interview inserts, tutorial steps, and other shots without a required visual handoff. These shots may be prompted in parallel.

## Actual-End Lock

For continuous work, inspect several frames near the ending and choose the last stable usable handoff, not automatically the encoded final frame. Lock:

- subject identity, wardrobe, props, damage, and transformation state
- screen position, pose, gaze, expression, movement direction, and action phase
- framing, camera position, height, axis side, and movement tail
- time, key-light direction, contrast, color relationship, and active practical lights
- location, weather, atmosphere, moving elements, and effects
- dialogue tail, ambience, synchronized sound, music state, or silence

Record deviations from the intended ending internally. The actual result wins.

## Handoff Rules

- Continue unfinished action from its current phase.
- Follow completed action with reaction, recovery, or consequence.
- Preserve screen direction unless a cut or camera move establishes a new axis.
- Carry sound across the cut or stop it deliberately.
- Motivate changes in light, weather, location, damage, and props.
- End on a state that is stable enough for editing or another continuation.

## Missing Evidence

Without a real result, do not claim verified continuity. The safe outputs are:

- the current shot prompt only
- a provider-neutral next-shot intention without executable timing
- a provisional opening labeled outside the prompt

Never invent an actual end state to keep the workflow moving.
